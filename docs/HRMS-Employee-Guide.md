# Employee Handbook

*Your complete guide to the company HR portal: what each screen does, how the rules that affect your pay actually work, and where to go when something needs fixing. Written for someone opening the system for the first time. Read it once and you will know your way around.*

---

## 1. Welcome

This is your company's **HRMS** — Human Resource Management System. It is the single place where your working life is recorded and managed:

- Marking attendance, applying for leave, and checking your shifts.
- Viewing payslips, requesting a loan or advance, and filing your tax declaration.
- Claiming expenses and travel, tracking goals, taking courses, and completing tasks.
- Reading announcements, answering surveys, and seeing each month's Rewards and Recognition winners.
- Keeping your profile and documents in order — and, when the time comes, submitting your resignation.

The system has two sides. **My Portal** is everything about you, and it is what this handbook covers. The **Admin Portal** is for HR, and for leadership. If you are a **Manager**, a few team features appear inside your own portal as well: approving your team's leave, and seeing who is present.

Everything here works in a **web browser** and in the **Android app**. Almost every day-to-day task is available on both.

[!TIP] The daily loop, in five minutes: sign in, punch in from **Attendance**, glance at your **Leave** balance, check **Announcements**, and read any notification waiting for you. That covers most days.

---

## 2. Signing in

Log in with the email address and password HR issued to you. New joiners are usually given a temporary password — change it as soon as you can.

If you have forgotten your password, use **Request a password reset** on the login screen. HR will reset it for you, and the reset signs you out everywhere, so you will log back in fresh on every device.

Your **role** decides what you see. Most people are an Employee. Some are also a Manager, which adds team approvals and a presence view. HR and leadership work in the Admin Portal.

[!WARNING] You cannot change your own login email from inside the app. Raise a **Change Request** (section 12) and HR will apply it for you.

## 3. Your dashboard

The dashboard is your landing page and works as a set of shortcuts. Tapping any card opens the full module behind it. From top to bottom you will usually see:

- A **welcome banner** with two quick figures — pending requests and leave days left — and buttons to add a request or apply for leave.
- **Announcements**, which you can dismiss individually. A dismissed notice stays dismissed for you.
- The **Rewards and Recognition** banner during the days after HR announces the month's winners.
- **Surveys** awaiting your response, and **interviews** you are scheduled to conduct.
- **Wishes** colleagues have sent you for a birthday or work anniversary.
- Your **attendance heatmap** — a calendar-style grid of the year so far.
- **Snapshot cards**: leave remaining, pending requests, and your employee code, designation and department.
- If you manage people, a **team status** strip showing who is present.

[!NOTE] Your net pay is deliberately not shown on the dashboard. Salary figures live on the Payslips screen only, so nothing sensitive is on display when someone glances at your phone.

## 4. Attendance

### 4.1 Punching in and out

Attendance is the record that you were at work, and it feeds directly into your pay. You get **one check-in and one check-out per day**.

- Every punch requires a **selfie**. This is mandatory, not optional.
- Every punch also captures your **GPS location**. The app shows only that it is working; your coordinates are never displayed, and you are never told whether you are inside or outside the office range. A punch is **never blocked** — the location is simply recorded for HR.
- **Work From Home** is a permission, not a checkbox everyone has. If it has been granted to you, a WFH tick appears at punch time and those punches are exempt from the office-range check. If you do not see it, it has not been granted.
- You may declare a **half day** at either punch.
- After check-in, a live timer counts your working time and freezes when you check out.

[!IMPORTANT] A day worth **under six hours** is recorded as a Half Day. If you forget to punch out, the day is counted from your check-in to **7:00 PM** and the same six-hour rule applies — so checking in at 10:00 AM and forgetting to punch out still counts as a full day, while checking in at 3:00 PM does not. If the times are wrong, raise a Regularization (section 4.4); once approved, the day is recalculated.

Your history sits below, filtered by month: status for each day (Present, Absent, Half Day, Weekly Off, Holiday, On Leave), your in and out times, hours worked, how late you were, and any remarks.

[!WARNING] "Late" means checking in after **10:00 AM**. This affects your pay — see section 5.

### 4.2 Working on a day you are on leave

If you punch in on a day you are already on approved leave, the system will not quietly turn it into an ordinary working day. Instead:

1. You are **warned before you punch**, and the punch is still allowed — you may genuinely be needed.
2. The day **stays recorded as leave** and your punch waits for approval from the top of your leave approval hierarchy.
3. When it is **approved**, the leave day is **given back to you** and the day counts as worked.
4. If it is **not approved**, the day remains leave and your punches stay on the record for reference.

HR is notified of the outcome either way.

[!NOTE] This does not apply to a half-day leave — you are expected to work the other half — nor to a Sunday or a company holiday, which are covered by the double-pay rule instead.

### 4.3 The "Lateness and leave" card

This card on your Attendance screen is the most useful thing on the page, because it shows what the month is costing or earning you while there is still time to change it:

- **Late arrivals** so far, against your free allowance.
- **Expected late deduction**, with the arithmetic behind it.
- **Paid leave used**, against your monthly quota.
- **Leave incentive** — extra pay you are on track to earn for unused paid leave.
- **No-punch days**, which are unpaid until regularised.
- **Sunday and comp-off duty** — rest days you actually worked, each paid double once approved.

[!NOTE] These figures are live estimates. The final amounts are fixed when HR runs and approves payroll.

### 4.4 Regularization

Use this to correct a missed or mistaken punch. Choose a date and a type — Missing Punch, Wrong Time, Forgot Check-in, Forgot Check-out, On Duty, or Other — enter the correct times, and give a reason.

Track it in the history table as it moves from Pending to Approved or Rejected, with the reviewer's note. On approval, that day's attendance is corrected automatically and its status recalculated.

### 4.5 My Shifts

A read-only list of your rostered shifts: date, shift name, and timing. Nothing to do here except check when you are scheduled.

## 5. The two pay rules worth knowing

Two rules are applied automatically every month and have a direct effect on your salary. They are worth understanding properly.

### Rule one — two paid leave days a month

Every month you are given **two paid leave days**.

- Take **two or fewer** approved leaves and those days are paid as normal.
- Take **more than two** and each additional day is **Loss of Pay**: that day's salary is deducted.
- Take **fewer than two** and the unused days are converted into extra pay for that month, called **Leave Incentive** — one day's salary for each unused day.

This is settled monthly. Unused leave does **not** carry forward. In the month you join or leave, the quota is prorated to the days you were actually on the payroll, and your Leave page always shows the quota that applies to you.

How it plays out:

- **No leave taken** — two extra days of pay.
- **One day taken** — one extra day of pay.
- **Two days taken** — normal pay, no bonus and no deduction.
- **Three days taken** — two are paid, and one day is unpaid.

### Rule two — five free late arrivals a month

A late arrival is a check-in after **10:00 AM**. The first **five each month are free**. From the sixth onward, each late day costs:

- **₹200 per day** if your monthly Basic salary is below ₹25,000, or
- **₹400 per day** if your monthly Basic is ₹25,000 or more.

In the month you join or leave, the five free days are prorated to the part of the month you were here.

How it plays out:

- **Late four times** — no penalty.
- **Late eight times** on a Basic of ₹20,000 — three chargeable days at ₹200, so ₹600.
- **Late six times** on a Basic of ₹30,000 — one chargeable day at ₹400, so ₹400.

### Where these appear

During the month, on the "Lateness and leave" card. In your salary, as a **Leave Incentive** line under earnings and a **Late coming** line under deductions.

[!IMPORTANT] Your Basic pay is never cut. Every earning on your payslip is the full monthly amount; days not worked and late arrivals are shown as **deductions** instead, so you can always see exactly what was taken and why.

## 6. Leave

### 6.1 Types of leave

There are four kinds, and the name you pick is the name that appears everywhere afterwards:

- **Paid Leave** — the ordinary request. Paid while your two-days-a-month quota lasts; anything beyond it is automatically unpaid.
- **Unpaid Leave** — explicitly unpaid, and it never touches the paid quota.
- **Emergency Leave** — granted the moment you file it, with no approval step. Your reporting hierarchy and HR are informed rather than asked, and it is paid exactly like Paid Leave.
- **Maternity Leave** — the statutory 26-week entitlement. Always paid, exempt from the monthly quota, and drawn from its own balance.

[!WARNING] Emergency leave is meant for genuine emergencies. From the second one in a single calendar month it is flagged to your managers and to HR as a repeat, and any of them may then charge that day at double the usual deduction.

### 6.2 Applying

Choose the type, set the dates, and add a reason. A **half day** counts as 0.5 and must start and end on the same date; a full request counts every day inclusive. Before you submit, the form shows how much of the request will be paid and how much falls outside your quota.

Your requests list shows the type, dates, day count, and status — Pending, Approved, Rejected or Cancelled — along with a visual of the approval chain and any note the decider left. You may cancel a request while it is pending, and even after approval; cancelling an approved leave returns the days to you.

### 6.3 How approval works

Your request climbs a ladder. If HR has configured a specific approval hierarchy for you, it follows that, one step at a time. Otherwise it walks up your reporting managers, stopping at the first CEO or MD. HR is kept informed throughout, and if you have no manager at all, HR decides.

You are notified at every step, not just at the outcome.

[!NOTE] Once a leave is fully approved, the covered days are written onto your attendance calendar as **On Leave** automatically. Sundays and holidays are skipped, and any day you actually worked is left alone. This is also what feeds the two-paid-leave rule in section 5.

### 6.4 Holidays

There is no separate holidays page. Company holidays appear in the **Calendar** and are automatically respected by attendance and by the pay rules.

## 7. Pay and money

### 7.1 Payslips

Your payslips are listed by period with gross, deductions, net, and status. Opening one shows the full breakdown: working, payable, unpaid, half and late days; every earning including Leave Incentive; every deduction including Loss of Pay and Late coming; and the payment date and reference.

Downloading the PDF works in two steps: you **request** the payslip, and HR **releases** it. This lets HR check and correct a slip before it goes out. Once released, the PDF is yours to download and it carries your statutory and bank details, the day counts, the earnings and deductions tables, and the net amount in words.

[!NOTE] If HR edits a payslip after releasing it, the download is withdrawn until it is released again. That is deliberate — it stops an outdated slip circulating.

### 7.2 Loans and advances

Request a Salary Advance, Personal Loan, Emergency loan, or Other, giving the principal and a reason. Track it through Pending, Approved, Active, and Closed. HR sets the EMI, tenure and balance; once active, the EMI is deducted from your salary automatically and appears on your payslip as Loan Recovery.

### 7.3 Tax declaration

Choose the financial year and your tax regime, then enter your investments and expenses by section — 80C, 80CCD(1B), 80D, 24B, 80E, 80G, HRA rent, LTA and Other. A running total updates as you type, and you can attach proof links.

Save a draft as often as you like. Once **submitted**, the form becomes read-only while HR verifies or rejects it with a note.

### 7.4 Expenses

Claim against a category — Travel, Food, Accommodation, Supplies, Medical, Communication, Other — with the amount, the date, the merchant, and a description.

[!IMPORTANT] A receipt is required. Upload the image or PDF with the claim; it is what HR verifies the payment against, and it is carried through to the accounts entry when you are reimbursed.

Track the claim through Pending, Approved, and Reimbursed, with the reviewer's note.

### 7.5 Travel

Raise a request with the purpose, origin and destination, dates, mode of travel, estimated cost, any advance needed, and notes. If you have already paid, complete the reimbursement block and attach the bill. The request status and the reimbursement status are tracked separately.

## 8. Growth

### 8.1 Goals

Goals assigned to you, with their period and status. You update your progress on a nought-to-hundred scale; your manager's note and rating appear once given.

### 8.2 Reviews

**Reviews assigned to you** are filled in by scoring each competency out of five, adding comments, an overall rating, strengths, and areas to improve.

**Feedback about you** is shown **anonymously** — you see the scores and the written comments, never who wrote them.

### 8.3 Learning

**My Courses** lists your approved enrolments with status, deadline, and progress. **Course Catalog** is everything on offer; requesting a course sends an enrolment request for approval, so you may see "awaiting approval" for a while.

The **course player** shows the video alongside the curriculum and tracks your progress as you go. Progress is measured on genuine watch time, so skipping ahead will not complete a module. If something is wrong with a course, use **Report an issue**.

## 9. Work and resources

### 9.1 Tasks

Tasks assigned to you with a project, priority, and due date. You move each one through Todo, In Progress, Review, and Done.

### 9.2 Assets

A read-only list of company property issued to you: name, serial, tag, category, and the date it was assigned.

### 9.3 Documents

A banner tells you which required documents are still missing. Upload one by picking a category and choosing files — PDF, JPG, PNG, WEBP, HEIC, DOC or DOCX, up to 5 MB each — with an optional note.

Each document is tracked from Submitted to Verified or Rejected, with the reviewer's note. You can download or delete the ones you uploaded. Some categories, such as your offer letter and appraisal letters, are uploaded by HR rather than by you.

[!TIP] If HR sends you a document-collection link, you can upload through it without logging in at all — useful before your account exists, or from a phone that is not signed in.

## 10. Communication

### 10.1 Announcements

Company notices carrying a category, a pinned marker, the author and the date. Dismissing one from your dashboard banner keeps it dismissed for you.

### 10.2 Surveys

Active surveys and polls appear as cards with a question count, and an anonymous tag where it applies. One response per person; answered surveys are marked as submitted.

### 10.3 Calendar

A month grid of everything happening: holidays, events, birthdays, work anniversaries, and interviews, colour-coded. Click a day for its detail.

### 10.4 Chat

Direct and group messaging, including video-call links. Chat is an organisation-wide switch controlled centrally — when it is off, the chat button and tab disappear entirely. Nothing is deleted; if it is switched back on, every past conversation returns exactly as it was.

### 10.5 Rewards and Recognition

Each month HR recognises an **Employee of the Month** for the whole company and a **Key Achiever** for each department. The winners appear as a banner on your dashboard with their photographs, stay for about two working days, and can be closed at any time. You are notified when they are announced.

## 11. If you approve, lead, or interview

These appear only when they are relevant to you:

- **Approvals** — if you are anyone's reporting manager, or sit in someone's approval hierarchy, their requests land here for your decision. This covers leave, attendance regularizations, work on a leave day, resignations, and no-dues clearance.
- **My Team** — for managers, a presence board of your direct reports showing who is in, on leave, or absent, with times, WFH tags and hours.
- **My Interviews** — rounds where you are the interviewer. Join the meeting, read the résumé, set the result, and save your feedback.
- **Onboarding** — your joining checklist, which you move through as you go.
- **Org Chart** — a visual of the company's reporting structure.

## 12. Your profile and requests

### 12.1 Profile

Your full record: personal details, employment information, statutory identifiers with the sensitive ones masked, and bank details.

You can change exactly two things yourself: your **profile photograph** and your **date of birth**. Everything else goes through a change request.

### 12.2 Change requests

Pick the field, see its current value, enter what it should be, and give a reason. Track it from pending to approved or declined, with HR's note. On approval HR applies the change for you.

### 12.3 Complaints

Raise a confidential complaint by choosing the person it concerns and describing the issue. It is visible only to leadership and HR, and **never** to the person it is about. Notifications about it are deliberately vague for the same reason. Track it through open, under review, resolved, or dismissed.

---

## 13. Leaving

Submit your resignation with your intended last working day, your notice period, and a reason, which is kept confidential.

Your resignation then climbs the same reporting hierarchy for approval. Once approved you enter your **notice period** — your login stays active and you continue working normally — while the no-dues clearance is completed by the departments that hold company property or access. Your account is deactivated only after clearance is done and your last working day has passed.

You can see the status throughout, along with your HR contact. You cannot open a second resignation while one is already in progress.

## 14. The mobile app

Almost everything above is in the Android app, organised under a **Menu**:

- **Time and Attendance** — Leave, Attendance, Regularization, My Roster.
- **Money** — Payslips, Expenses, Travel, Loans.
- **Growth** — Tasks, My Interviews, Goals, Reviews, Learning.
- **Requests and lifecycle** — My Approvals, Change Requests, Complaints, Tax Declaration, Onboarding, Resignation.
- **Workplace** — Announcements, Surveys, Documents, Assets, Org Chart, and this guide.

**Calendar** and **Notifications** are tabs of their own, and a **search** button in the middle of the tab bar jumps to any screen or looks up a colleague.

[!TIP] Turn on notifications when the app asks. Punch-in and punch-out reminders, approval requests, and announcements all arrive that way, and they are the difference between remembering to punch out and losing the day's hours.

## 15. Quick answers

- **How do I mark attendance?** Attendance, then Check In with a selfie, and Check Out when you leave.
- **When am I late?** Any check-in after 10:00 AM.
- **How many free late arrivals?** Five a month. After that, ₹200 or ₹400 a day depending on your Basic.
- **How much paid leave?** Two days a month. Unused days become extra pay; extra days are unpaid. Nothing carries forward.
- **Where do I see what this month will cost me?** The "Lateness and leave" card on the Attendance screen.
- **What if I work on a day I am on leave?** Punch in as normal. The day stays leave until your approver confirms it, then the leave day is returned to you.
- **Why can I not download my payslip?** Request it first; HR releases it, and then the download appears.
- **What can I edit myself?** Your profile photograph and your date of birth. Everything else is a change request.
- **Who sees my complaint?** Leadership and HR only — never the person it concerns.
- **Something in a course is broken.** Use "Report an issue" inside the course player.
